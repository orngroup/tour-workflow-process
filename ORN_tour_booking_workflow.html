Phase 1 — Enquiry & sale
1
Customer enquiry received
WhatsApp, call, walk-in, agent referral — not a booking
Sales
An enquiry, verbal interest, or partial interest is not a confirmed booking. No system entry, no commitment, no inventory block at this stage.
2
Ground reality disclosure
Hotels, rooms, meals, transfers, group discipline — disclosed before payment
Sales
Covers: hotel may be outside city centre · room types limited to single/twin/double/triple · triples may include extra/sofa bed · meals may be packed · transfers are common group · group timings are fixed · walking involved. Must be acknowledged and recorded before Step 3.
Gate A — Disclosure gate
Hard stop ›
Condition: Ground reality acknowledgement must be signed/recorded by every traveller before any payment is accepted.
If failed: Booking Owner may not proceed. Disc. not complete = file not quality-cleared. Sales-side failure, not Ops problem.

Sales

Gate B — Booking gate
Hard stop ›
  • Booking amount received (mandatory)
  • Passenger names as per passport (mandatory)
  • Passport copies collected (mandatory)
  • Departure date selected (mandatory)
  • Package version locked (mandatory)
  • Rooming type recorded (mandatory)
  • Payment schedule communicated (mandatory)
  • Visa requirement explained (mandatory)
  • Cancellation terms shared (mandatory)
  • Discount/special price recorded if any (mandatory)

If any item missing: File status = "Incomplete Booking" — NOT "Operationally Ready". Payment alone ≠ confirmed booking.

Booking Owner
If passport, contact, rooming, visa-relevant data are not collected → booking remains Incomplete. Rule of thumb: two or more "tbd" fields = enquiry with money attached, not a confirmed booking.

Ticketing

Gate C — Visa gate
Hard stop ›
Condition: Visa fee + required package deposit received before visa process may begin.
Late booking rule: If visa feasibility cannot be confirmed → booking must not proceed for Europe. §08: "No visa feasibility, no late Europe booking."
Escalation trigger: Passport not in hand at T-45 → escalate to Booking Owner and Ops.
Gate D — Flight gate
Hard stop ›
Condition: Required ticketing deposit received before flights may be sealed.
Name rule: Flight name must exactly match passport. No deviation. Booking Owner responsible for name match verification.
If flight not sealed by T-30: Escalation to Ops and management.
Phase 4 — Financial closure
6
Supplier gate — Gate E
Minimum package collection received · supplier commitments may be protected
Finance
Booking Owner
§06 Rule: Company shall not spend irreversible money before customer has paid the required milestone. No supplier deposits, no hotel blocks, no coach contracts before Gate E clears.
Gate E — Final payment gate
Hard stop ›
Condition: 100% package amount + TCS + supplements received. Zero dues pending.
Consequence: No passenger may enter final operations handover with outstanding dues.
Escalation: Outstanding dues at T-14 → Finance escalates to Booking Owner and management. Repeat offenders → Booking Owner scorecard entry (§25).
Phase 5 — Group strength & freeze
7
Group strength review
Minimum passenger threshold assessed · departure go/hold/merge decision
Operations
Sales
§18: Group strength and departure freeze gate. Decision: proceed · hold for more bookings · merge with adjacent departure · cancel. Sales must communicate any change to all passengers immediately.
Gate F — Freeze gate (warning)
Escalation ›
Trigger: Departure below minimum strength at freeze deadline.
Options: (1) Wait — extend window with risk · (2) Merge with nearest departure · (3) Cancel with full refund process. No option may be selected without management sign-off.
§20 Operational freeze: Once departure is frozen, no new bookings, no changes without Ops approval.

Sales-to-Operations handover

Booking Owner
Operations
§21 Handover checklist: passenger list with passport · rooming plan · meal & dietary list · flight PNRs · visa status per pax · optionals booked · special requests approved · ground reality acknowledgements on file · payment status = zero dues · agent details if applicable. Operations may refuse handover if file is incomplete.
Gate G — Handover acceptance gate
Hard stop ›
Ops may reject handover if:
  • Any mandatory field missing from passenger file
  • Ground reality acknowledgement not on file
  • Rooming not finalised
  • Outstanding dues exist
  • Visa status unresolved
  • Flight PNR not confirmed
  • Special request not in writing with approval
Rejected handover = Sales-side failure. Booking Owner remains accountable until Ops formally accepts.

Error states & escalation triggers
Booking error accountability (§23)
Escalation ›
Any preventable booking error that becomes an on-road crisis is the Booking Owner's accountability.

Triggers immediate escalation: Name mismatch on flight · missing visa · undisclosed special request denied on-road · rooming error causing hotel complaint · unreported medical need causing incident.
Non-negotiable violations (§26)
Zero tolerance ›
  • Unrecorded discount or price given to customer
  • Verbal inclusion commitment not in writing
  • Booking marked confirmed without payment received
  • Dirty file pushed to Operations
  • Booking Owner responsibility transferred without documented handoff note
  • Agent booking confirmed without written terms
  • No visa feasibility confirmed for late Europe booking
  • Vouchers released with outstanding dues
Escalation process (§24)
Process ›
Level 1: Booking Owner → Ops & Sales Control desk · 24hr resolution window
Level 2: Ops & Sales Control → Management · 4hr escalation if unresolved
Level 3: Management decision — final · documented in file

§25 Booking Owner Scorecard tracks: errors per season · late collections · dirty handovers · customer complaints attributable to booking stage. Reviewed each season.
The simple rule from which everything else descends:
Sell clean · Book clean · Collect on time · Freeze early · Handover complete · Operate without surprises
